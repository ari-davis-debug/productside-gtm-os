## What goes here

- **Why pure-external alphas.** I have zero access to Productside's CRM, LMS, or alumni history. That's a feature, not a bug — every alpha here could be running on day one without a single integration. If hired, the proprietary data is a multiplier on top, not a prerequisite.
- **Why a training company is a great fit for the pattern.** Buyer (VP/CPO of Product) is publicly identifiable. Buying triggers (new PM hire, team scaling, exec change, M&A, capability-gap admission) leave public footprints. Competitor set is small and well-documented. Most outbound shops in this space are still buying generic lists.
- **What "AE-resonant" means.** Three motions an account exec already knows — hand-raise, trigger, pain. Each of the seven alphas maps to one of those three. The job isn't to invent a new sales motion. It's to feed the existing one cleaner inputs.
- **The Productside-specific moment.** Brand transition from 280 Group → Productside is still finishing. That moment itself is an alpha (backlink recovery). The fact that the moment is happening *now* is why a GTM Engineer role exists.
